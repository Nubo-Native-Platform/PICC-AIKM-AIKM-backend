# Code of Conduct

Nubo Native Platform (NNP) follows the [CNCF Community Code of Conduct](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md).

As contributors and maintainers of **PICC-AIKM-AIKM-backend**, and in the interest of fostering an open and welcoming community, we pledge to respect all people who contribute through reporting issues, requesting features, improving documentation, submitting pull requests, reviewing changes, testing releases, and supporting operations.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the maintainers at **contribution@nubons.com**.

The full policy text we adhere to is maintained by the CNCF at the link above.
